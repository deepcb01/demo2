AI Product Design

Discovery, journeys, prototypes and validation for AI-powered experiences.

Overview

What is AI Product Design?

AI products raise design questions that conventional software does not: how do people know when to trust an answer, what happens when the system is unsure, and how can they correct it? VIGNOVATE designs AI experiences with those questions in mind from the first sketch.

We work from user needs to journeys, wireframes and interactive prototypes, and test them with representative users before development begins.

Problems it can address

  • Users do not understand what the AI can and cannot do.
  • Interfaces hide uncertainty or errors.
  • Teams build before validating the experience.
  • Designs are difficult for developers to implement.

Suitable use cases

  • Designing a new AI assistant or copilot
  • Redesigning a confusing AI feature
  • Prototyping an idea before investing in development
  • Creating a design system for a SaaS product
  • Testing a flow with real users

Honest limits

What to keep in mind

  • Prototypes demonstrate an idea; they are not production software.
  • Testing with a few users reveals problems but does not prove market demand.
  • AI behaviour can only be partly simulated in a prototype.

FAQ

AI Product Design questions

Do I need design before development?

It reduces risk. Even a lightweight prototype can reveal problems cheaply, before they are expensive to change.

Can you design without building the product?

Yes. We can deliver designs and prototypes for your own team or another developer to build.

How do you validate a design?

With task-based tests on a prototype, using representative users where possible, and by reviewing the results together.
